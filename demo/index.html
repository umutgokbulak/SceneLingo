SceneLingo · live demo

The browser extension, running on a short film without being installed. Press play: the subtitles come in English and in your language. Point at a word for its meaning and its sound; click it for the dictionary, and save it with the star. The bar over the subtitles has the rest: a line again, slower, the film paused after each line, writing or saying what you hear.
